Landon Frost

Owner & Head Coach · Frost Elite Sports

I'm a former multi-sport, scholarship-level athlete turned performance trainer, focused on helping baseball and softball players improve their skills and reach their potential. With experience in high-level competition, advanced instruction, and hands-on coaching, I work to provide athletes with practical, detail-oriented training in a supportive, high-energy environment.

My playing success led to scholarship offers from multiple schools across different divisions. After an injury shifted my path, I continued my career at Corban University, where I was a four-year starting outfielder, earned the Cascade Conference Golden Glove Award, and played for the Bend Elks of the West Coast League to pursue getting drafted. I graduated in 2016 with a degree in Business and Sports Management.

Samantha Frost

Founder · Head Coach · Pitching Specialist

Softball has been my passion for as long as I can remember, shaping who I am both on and off the field. Growing up in Clackamas and playing at Clackamas High School, I poured every ounce of energy into the game, earning Pitcher of the Year honors all four years, multiple All-Conference and All-State accolades, and setting the school's strikeout record. Every pitch, every game, and every challenge fueled my drive to compete at the highest level and inspired me to help others reach their full potential.

After college, I spent eight years coaching at Rex Putnam High School, where I had the privilege of guiding young athletes, building championship teams, and creating a culture of dedication, teamwork, and relentless improvement. Today, I am proud to be the head coach at Clackamas High School, returning to my roots to give back to the community that shaped my love for the game.

Coaching isn't just a career for me — it's a calling. There's nothing more rewarding than watching athletes develop confidence, passion, and skills that carry them far beyond the softball field.
