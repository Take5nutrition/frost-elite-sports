The Details

What We Train — and Why It Matters.

Training at Frost Elite is built around details that win innings: efficient movement, repeatable mechanics, and decision-making under pressure. Athletes don't just "get reps" — they learn how to execute.

Hitting
Swing decisions, timing, bat path, approach, and confidence in the box.
Defense
Footwork, first-step quickness, angles, glove work, and clean transfers.
Catching
Receiving, blocking, throwdowns, and game-control fundamentals.
Pitching
Mechanics, command, movement, sequencing, and composure.
Movement
Strength base, stability, and efficient patterns to reduce breakdowns.
Durability
Training that supports healthy workload and long-term athletic development.
